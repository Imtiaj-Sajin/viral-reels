===== INSTAGRAM / FACEBOOK CAPTION (paste as is) =====
Your AI isn't ignoring you. It's running out of memory. 🧠

Every chat has a limit called a context window. When it fills up, the oldest messages get pushed out, so the rules you gave it an hour ago quietly disappear.

The 10-second fix. Paste this into your long chat:

"Summarize everything important from this chat: my goal, the rules I gave you, the decisions we made and any open questions. Keep it under 200 words."

Copy the summary, open a new chat, paste it in. Fresh memory, same brain.

Send this to the friend who's still yelling at their AI 😅
