In 1971 a man hijacked a plane, took $200,000 and jumped out into the night. 55 years later, nobody knows who he was.

🎟️ paid $20 cash for his ticket and signed it "Dan Cooper"
📝 handed the flight attendant a note. She thought it was his phone number
💼 a briefcase with red cylinders and wires
🪂 asked for 4 parachutes. One was a dummy, sewn shut. It vanished with him
🌧️ jumped from the rear stairs at night, in the rain, in a trench coat and loafers
💵 the FBI recorded every serial number. Not one bill was ever spent
🏖️ 1980: an 8-year-old found $5,800 of it buried on a Columbia River beach, still in rubber bands
📁 2016: the FBI suspended the case. Still the only unsolved hijacking in airline history

Did he survive the jump? Drop your theory 👇
